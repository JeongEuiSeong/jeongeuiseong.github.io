Privacy Policy for Daram

Last Updated: November 15, 2026
Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Daram ("the App", "we", "our") values and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Daram iOS application.

1. Core Principle: On-Device & Serverless

Daram is designed with privacy-first principles:

  • No External Backend Servers: We do not operate external database servers that store or collect your financial or personal transactions.
  • On-Device Storage: Your ledger entries (expenses, income, amounts, categories, payment methods, notes) are stored locally on your device using encrypted Apple frameworks (Core Data).
  • On-Device Parsing: Photos of receipts, card statements, or SMS messages are processed locally on your device using Apple's Vision and text parsing algorithms. No images or text copies are transmitted to external servers.
  • Recognition Failure Reports (only when you send them): If Daram cannot read a payment SMS or screenshot, you can tap "Report to developer" to open a pre-filled email in your Mail app. The SMS text is included with digits and names masked, and screenshots are attached as-is. Nothing is sent unless you review the email and tap Send yourself; the app never sends it automatically. Reports go to the developer's email (jes8076@gmail.com), are used only to improve recognition, and are deleted once that work is done. You may request deletion at any time by email.

2. Information Collected and Processed

A. Ledger and Financial Records (User Data)

  • Data processed: Amounts, dates, categories, payment methods, merchant names, custom notes, and budget configurations.
  • Where stored: Locally on your iOS device.
  • iCloud Sync and Sharing (Optional): If you choose to enable iCloud synchronization or shared ledgers, your data is securely stored and synchronized through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) service under your own Apple ID account. The developer cannot access or view your private iCloud data.

B. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

  • Data processed: Subscription status (e.g., Daram Premium), transaction date, anonymous receipt tokens.
  • Processor: Apple Inc. (StoreKit).
  • We do not collect or store your credit card or financial billing information. All purchase transactions are handled securely by Apple.

C. Advertising and Diagnostics (Free Version Only)

The free version of Daram uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display banner, native, and rewarded ads.

  • Data collected by Google:
    • Device Identifiers: Advertising Identifier (IDFA, only if you explicitly grant permission via App Tracking Transparency), Identifier for Vendors (IDFV).
    • Diagnostic & Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, performance, and crash logs.
    • Device Information: OS version, device model, and approximate location (derived from IP address).
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): You can choose whether to allow tracking for personalized ads. If you decline, you will still enjoy all free features with non-personalized ads.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Third-party integrations are limited to verified platform services:

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Deleting the Daram application from your device immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored data.
  • iCloud Data: You can manage and delete your synchronized iCloud data anytime via iOS Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage > Daram.

5. User Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • You may view, edit, or delete any ledger transaction or category directly within the app at any time.
  • You can revoke tracking permissions anytime under iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can disable iCloud sync anytime in the app settings or iOS system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Daram does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 14.
